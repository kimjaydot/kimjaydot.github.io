About me

From GeoCities to UX

My journey into tech started when my friend Frank introduced me to gaming, from Duke Nukem on PC to Super Mario on Nintendo. Exploring GeoCities websites sparked my love of web design, and I taught myself Paint Shop Pro and web languages on createblog.com.

That curiosity led me to earn a BFA in Interactive Media Design and work as a web designer for seven years in corporate settings. A class in Human-Centered Design then shifted my focus to UX, inspiring me to earn my M.S. in User Experience at Kent State University.

Today I work on the front desk team at Banfield Pet Hospital, where I see firsthand how clients and veterinary teams experience pet care. Outside of work, I love belly dancing, time with family, and cuddling my talkative cat, Dixie.
